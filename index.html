A little about me

Engineering with people in mind

I'm a Biomedical Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University, with minors in Philosophy and Biological Psychology. I'm interested in how technology, design, and public policy can make people's lives better.

Through accessibility advocacy, student leadership, and interdisciplinary projects, I've become especially interested in how we design systems, technologies, and environments, and whether they actually work for the people using them.

Gait Analysis Using MATLAB

Who it's for: anyone using gait data to understand how a person walks.

Analysed plantar-pressure data from PhysioNet: centre-of-pressure paths, stance time, peak pressure, and gait asymmetry, plus an animated pressure visualization.
